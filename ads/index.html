Voices: put ELEVENLABS_API_KEY in the project .env, then cd ads/tools && node voice.mjs. Pick the voices in voice/voices.json (node voice.mjs --list shows yours). Serve this folder over http to hear them while previewing.

To make a video: open a Clean link, then record the window with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or Cmd+Shift+5. Add your voice-over or a trending sound in CapCut. These pages are not part of the website build and are not deployed.
